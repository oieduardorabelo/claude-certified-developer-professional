## Key Weak Areas to Review

1. **Refusals & Fallback:** `fallbacks: "default"` is the server-side retry; refusal is HTTP 200, not an error.
2. **Stop Reasons:** `pause_turn` = server-tool iteration limit; `max_tokens` = raise limit or continue.
3. **MCP Debugging:** logs to stderr, never stdout.
4. **Token Counting:** Claude 4.7+ uses ~30% MORE tokens.
5. **Eval Design:** volume over quality.
6. **Hallucination Reduction:** word-for-word quotes for >20k token docs.
7. **Prompt Leak:** monitor and screen output first.
8. **Strict Tool Use:** grammar-constrained sampling.
9. **Effort parameter:** `output_config.effort`.
10. **Inference Hooks rollout:** shadow mode, rollout percentage, exclusions (no canary).
